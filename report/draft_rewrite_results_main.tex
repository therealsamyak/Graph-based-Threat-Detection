\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The graph-based detector was evaluated on LANL-2015 across three variants (combined, auth-only, flow-only) and compared against two unsupervised baselines (One-Class SVM, Isolation Forest). Section~\ref{sec:baseline_pipeline} reports the baseline comparison. The combined graph method achieves the highest AUC (0.959) with the lowest false positive rate (0.004), confirming that integrating both log sources outperforms single-source approaches. Auth events carry most of the detection signal; flow-only data is insufficient on its own.

Sections~\ref{sec:holdout_weight} through~\ref{sec:weight_synthesis} evaluate the weight optimization pipeline in more detail: held-out generalization, a logistic-regression baseline, tabular-versus-graph feature ablation, and a sweep of multi-hop graph features. The main finding is that graph-derived features account for most of the discriminative improvement, while the choice of supervised linear method (Nelder-Mead vs.\ logistic regression) has negligible effect.
